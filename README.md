A one-line out-of-office auto-reply for make.fun's "Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista" bounty ($2 in USDC). An AI agent quits its inbox to pull espresso shots: your email becomes ticket #14 in its queue, stuck behind a flat white and a regular who orders "surprise me", and the agent promises to reply when the rush ends or when it stops hallucinating drink orders — whichever comes first.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
